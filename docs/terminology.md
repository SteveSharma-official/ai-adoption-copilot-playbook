# Terminology

| Term | Meaning | Book chapter | Lab |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copilot Enterprise Adoption Model | Five levels (L1 to L5), each gated by evidence | 1 | 1.1 |
| AI Adoption Decision Ladder | Buy, Configure, Integrate, Build, Host | 4 | 4.1 |

| Copilot Agent Authority Matrix | L1 Inform, L2 Recommend, L3 Act with approval, L4 Act autonomously, Never | 7 | 7.1 |
| Escalation Trigger Model | When an agent must hand to a person | 10 | 10.1, 16.1 |
| Use-Case Discovery Canvas | Ten criteria scored 0 to 2 with evidence; 15 Pilot, 10 Reframe | 9 | 9.1 |
| Copilot Production Readiness Gate | Six domains, bands 80 and 60 per cent, four hard gates | 12 | 12.2 |
| Citizen-to-Pro-Code Adoption Ladder | Rungs 1 Personal, 2 Team, 3 Departmental, 4 Enterprise | 17 | 17.1 |
| Restricted Content Discovery (RCD) | Hides a site from Copilot and search discovery; does not remove access | 6 | 6.1, 6.2 |
